Privacy policy

Makeover · AI Interior Studio, by Group 36 (Outskill AI Accelerator). Last updated 31 October 2026.

What we keep

  • Guests: a random code saved in your browser, your token balance, and the designs you generate.
  • If you sign in with Google: your name, email address and profile picture from Google, so we can show your account and keep your designs and tokens across devices. We do not get your Google password or access to anything else in your Google account.
  • Designs: the redesigned images, a small preview, and the details you choose (style, room, name and note).

Your room photos

To create a design, your photo is sent to Google Gemini through our server. We do not store your original photo; we keep only the designs it produces.

Where it is stored

On Cloudflare (D1 database and Workers KV), used only to run Makeover. We do not sell or share your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Your choices

You can delete any design from My designs at any time. To delete your account and everything in it, email atef.nansi@gmail.com.
